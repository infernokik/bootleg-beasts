**Ante modes ("feature spins").** Hacksaw sells a per-spin boosted mode next to the bonus buys. The most
common listed is a 3× bet spin that makes the bonus **5× more likely to trigger**
([Sigma, Epic Bullets and Bounty](https://sigma.world/play/games/hacksaw-gaming/epic-bullets-and-bounty/),
[SlotCatalog, Great Game Rockies](https://slotcatalog.com/en/slots/great-game-rockies)). Some games add a
premium feature spin, e.g. a 50× spin that guarantees a special symbol. These are toggles beside the grid, not
a menu buried in settings.

**Bonus buy tiers.** Typically 2–5 options in one menu, priced roughly 50×–400× bet, each with its own RTP
shown (usually within a few tenths of a percent of base). The menu opens from a large yellow "BUY BONUS"
button ([SlotCatalog, Le Bandit](https://slotcatalog.com/en/slots/Le-Bandit)).

**Hidden epic bonus.** Many recent Hacksaw games have a third, unadvertised-in-the-buy-menu bonus that only
5 scatters can trigger, usually 10 spins with an upgraded mechanic; 3 and 4 scatters trigger the normal
bonuses ([iGaming Today, The Wildwood Curse](https://www.igamingtoday.com/the-wildwood-curse-slot-review/),
[iGaming Today, Secret Order](https://www.igamingtoday.com/secret-order-slot-review/)). The top tier is
deliberately not purchasable, so it stays an earned moment.

**Recurring mechanics.** Sticky wilds and sticky multipliers in free spins, mystery multiplier wilds, "collect"
symbols that gather coin values, and guaranteed-feature buys ("each spin has a wild").

**UI and presentation (observed, partly unverified).** A thin bottom bar with balance, bet, spin, autoplay and
turbo; a prominent buy button to the left of the grid; short skippable bonus intros; escalating win-tier
banners with a count-up. Hacksaw does not publish max-win odds
([Casinos.com](https://www.casinos.com/slots/hand-of-anubis)). No source documented their exact big-win tier
thresholds or audio approach.

**Mechanic families (described generically).**
- *Expanding ways:* a mystery symbol expands into 2–4 copies of the same symbol, multiplying ways.
- *Splitting symbols:* a symbol halves every symbol in its row or reel, doubling counts and ways.
- *Nudging wilds:* a stacked wild nudges until fully visible, and each nudge adds +1 to its multiplier.
- *Exploding wilds:* a wild destroys neighbours, new symbols drop in, and a multiplier grows per explosion.
- *Enhancer cells:* special cells under the grid that open when enough frames are collected and reveal
  a modifier ([Casinos.com](https://www.casinos.com/nolimit-city), [OLBG, Mental](https://www.olbg.com/slots/games/mental)).

**Buy families.** Feature boosters (buy a modifier for the base game) and bonus buys (skip to a round), with
prices up to ~2,000× ([casino guide via search](https://www.casinos.com/nolimit-city), **unverified**) and
outliers above that (Mental 2's 6,666× top buy). Many games have a "lucky draw" style buy that gambles
between a cheaper and a better bonus, and a premium "one shot at the top" buy. Several also add a hidden or
super version of the bonus reached by extra scatters or by upgrading during the round.

**Presentation.** Cinematic intros, gritty painted art, a bonus-selection or upgrade screen before free spins,
visible upgrade ladders ("collect X to unlock the next tier"), and dark humour that review sites call
borderline offensive ([Casinos.com](https://www.casinos.com/nolimit-city)).

## 3. US regulated market (NJ, PA, MI) and the UK for comparison

**The lab standard.** US states certify against [GLI-19 v3.0](https://gaminglabs.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/GLI-19-Interactive-Gaming-Systems-v3.0.pdf)
plus state rules (a v4.0 draft was released for comment in July 2026). Clauses that shape the UI:

- **4.3.4 Always on screen:** balance, current bet, wager options, and the last game's outcome and amount won.
- **4.4.1 Rules:** help must be reachable without a wager and describe every feature, autoplay, symbol
  transformation, min/max bets, all wins for every wager option, what multipliers apply to, which symbols are
  wild in each phase, symbols that only exist in the bonus, **any max-win cap** (4.4.1 r), and **any non-wager
  purchase and its value** (4.4.1 q, which covers bonus buys).
- **4.7.1–4.7.3 RTP and odds:** minimum 75% RTP for every wager mode; RTP display is at the regulator's choice
  but, if shown, must explain how it is reached and account for extra wagers. If the top advertised award is
  rarer than 1 in 100 million, its odds must be shown.
- **4.8.1 Bonus status:** show progress toward a collect-based feature (number needed and collected), make
  the bonus mode obvious, and show spins awarded and remaining.
- **4.9.2 Autoplay:** allowed if the jurisdiction enables it; player-set limits stay in force, the remaining
  count is shown, it stops on any limit, and the player can stop after the current game.
- **4.14 Game recall:** replay of the last game with date/time, bet, balance, win, any purchase, and **at
  least the last 50 events of a completed bonus**.

**New Jersey.** N.J.A.C. 13:69O-1.2 requires the software to either show the current time and session
elapsed time continuously or show a pop-up at least every 30 minutes, and to show the previous log-in time
([NJ DGE chapter 69O](https://www.nj.gov/lps/ge/docs/Regulations/CHAPTER69O.pdf)). A new responsible-gaming
section was adopted in October 2026 ([NJ DGE](https://www.nj.gov/oag/ge/docs/ProposedRules/oct2026/OnlineResponsibleGamingAdoption10192026.pdf);
details not reviewed). A net win/loss display rule for NJ was not confirmed **(unverified)**; operators often
provide it in the wrapper.

**Pennsylvania.** 58 Pa. Code 814a requires showing the player's local time and session elapsed time, and a
pop-up at least hourly with elapsed time and amount wagered; play must be recreatable for 10 years
([Justia 809a.6](https://regulations.justia.com/states/pennsylvania/title-58/part-vii/subpart-l/chapter-809a/section-809a-6),
[Cornell 814a.3](https://law.cornell.edu/regulations/pennsylvania/58-Pa-Code-SS-814a-3)).

**Michigan.** Also certifies to GLI-19 with its own rules; specific UI clauses were not reviewed **(unverified)**.

**Bonus buys in the US.** No source found a ban in NJ, PA or MI. Hard Rock Bet markets bonus buy slots to
NJ and MI players at typical prices of 50×–100× ([Hard Rock Bet](https://hardrock.bet/casino/how-to-play-slots/bonus-buy-slots/)),
and FanDuel promotes its own list of bonus buy slots ([FanDuel](https://www.fanduel.com/research/5-best-bonus-buy-slots-fanduel-casino)).
Fewer buy games are offered than offshore ([Casinos.com](https://www.casinos.com/news/best-new-slots-with-bonus-buy-features-in-the-us)).
Whether US versions of Hacksaw and Nolimit games keep every buy tier was not confirmed **(unverified)**.
A minimum spin time and an autoplay ban were not found for any US state.

**UK (strictest comparison).** Since 31 October 2021: minimum 2.5 s game cycle, no turbo/quick spin/slam stop,
no autoplay, no celebrating a return that is equal to or less than the stake, and net position and session time
shown ([UKGC RTS 14](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/standards/remote-gambling-and-software-technical-standards/rts-14-responsible-product-design),
[UKGC update](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/licensees-and-businesses/guide/page/updates-to-rts-effective-31-october-2021)).
Bonus buys are not offered in the UK (every review above notes "not UK"). Stakes are capped at £5 per spin
for players 25+ since April 2025 ([IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/mobile-first-design-online-slot-development-1825296)).

**Mobile.** Industry commentary treats portrait as the default for phone play because it can be driven with
one thumb, with main controls in the bottom third of the screen
([CasinoBeats](https://casinobeats.com/2021/05/28/one-handed-play-the-way-forward-for-mobile-slots/),
[Epicstream](https://epicstream.com/article/mobile-slot-ux-guide-portrait-mode-thumb-friendly-controls-and-animation-overload)).
No reliable public figure for the mobile share of US iGaming play was found. GLI-19 4.2.2 d allows a shortened
rules page on small screens if the full version is one tap away.

## 5. What Bootleg Beasts adopts

Ordered by impact on how "flagship" the game feels, weighed against effort. Feature names here are our own and
generic.

1. **Raise the max win to 10,000× and show its odds in the rules.** 5,000× is below every peer studied (Hacksaw's
   floor is about 10,000×). The cap is part of the pitch, and GLI-19 4.4.1 r requires disclosing it. Re-tune the
   Overload capture weights so the cap is reachable, and publish "1 in N" from the simulator.
2. **Add one ante toggle: ~3× bet for ~5× bonus trigger rate.** This matches the most common studio pattern
   and is the "optional extra" still open in the design spec. Implement it as a separate Core-heavy reel set, so
   the exact base formula still works, and tune it to 96% RTP. Show cost and odds on the toggle.
3. **Add a per-spin "guaranteed drop" feature spin.** For a fixed price (target 20×–50×), each spin gets a
   guaranteed wild cross. Because the base game is computed exactly, the price can be set exactly. It sells the
   game's signature moment directly, as premium feature spins do.
4. **Keep the top level unbuyable and give it an earned route.** Overload should only be reached by levelling
   up, or by a very rare trigger (for example 5 Cores plus a Reactor on the trigger spin). Both studios keep the
   best bonus out of the menu so it stays a story moment.
5. **Redesign the buy menu as cards.** One card per option: art, name, price in currency and ×bet, RTP,
   starting level, guaranteed minimum, and a volatility meter. Ask to confirm above a threshold (e.g. 200×).
   GLI-19 4.4.1 q requires the price and value of any non-wager purchase to be clear.
6. **Add a jurisdiction config.** Flags for buy menu, ante, feature spins, autoplay, turbo, a 2.5 s minimum cycle,
   no celebration at or below stake, and the RTP variant. One build can then show both "US (NJ/PA/MI)" and
   "UK" modes, which demonstrates compliance thinking to a studio.
7. **Show the compliance strip.** Clock and session elapsed time (NJ and PA), net position for the session,
   and the balance/bet/last-win meters from GLI-19 4.3.4, laid out without crowding the grid.
8. **Add game history with bonus replay.** Show the last N rounds with date/time, bet, win, balance, purchase,
   and a step-through of every free spin in a bonus (at least 50 events, GLI-19 4.14.3). The engine is
   deterministic, so storing seeds or results makes this cheap. It doubles as a debugging tool.
9. **Write full rules and paytable pages.** Cover the Static reveal weights' effect in words, wilds per phase,
   bonus-only symbols (Reactors, Override), what capture multipliers apply to, level ladder, guaranteed
   minimums, max-win cut-off, RTP per mode and per buy, and min/max bets. This meets GLI-19 4.4.1 and is what
   operators check first.
10. **Add autoplay to GLI-19 4.9.2.** Spin count, loss limit, single-win limit, stop on bonus, stop after the
    current game, counter shown, off by default, and removed by the jurisdiction flag.
11. **Rebuild the layout portrait-first.** Grid on top, level meter between grid and controls, and spin, bet ±,
    buy and ante in the bottom third within thumb reach. Rules and history go behind one menu button. Landscape
    can be derived from it.
12. **Tie win tiers to fixed thresholds and make them skippable.** For example Big 20×, Mega 50×, Epic 100×,
    Legendary 500×, with no celebration for wins at or below the stake, and a tap to skip the count-up (still
    within the minimum cycle where enforced). It keeps the current presentation but makes it predictable and
    UK-safe.
13. **Add a short bonus-selection screen for buys.** After a buy, show the level ladder with the starting level
    lit and the next unlock ("3 Reactors: +4 spins, columns lock"), then play the intro. It gives the upgrade-
    ladder feel and meets GLI-19 4.8.1 a–b (progress toward the next feature shown).
14. **Show a volatility rating and hit rate in the info screen.** Both studios label volatility, and players use
    it to choose buys. Report the simulated 22× SD as "very high" and the 30.8% hit rate.
15. **Decide volatility openly.** With a 10,000× cap, ante and feature spins, the game fits the high to
    very-high band where both studios sit. Close the "medium volatility" open item in the design spec rather
    than flattening captures.
